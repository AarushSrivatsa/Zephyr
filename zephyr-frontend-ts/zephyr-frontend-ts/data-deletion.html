Zephyr only stores data tied to your connected Instagram account — your rules, DM logs, and account info. Disconnecting deletes it permanently.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Zephyr immediately unsubscribes from your Instagram account's webhooks — no more comments are read or DMs sent.
  • All of your active sessions are signed out and your session tokens are deleted right away.
  • Your stored Instagram access token is removed immediately.
  • Your account record, rules, and comment/DM logs are then permanently deleted from our database within 30 days.
